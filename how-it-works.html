Process Lifecycle

Platform Operation & Workflow

A transparent, role-based architecture serving Students, Recruiters, and the Training & Placement Cell.

1. Student Workflow

Step 1: Students register their university roll number, branch, and academic record.
Step 2: Build complete profile with validated skills, GitHub project links, certifications, and uploaded PDF resume.
Step 3: The smart matching system automatically evaluates active jobs against the student's profile, computing match criteria and skill fit.
Step 4: Apply to eligible jobs, track interview rounds, and review official placement offers.

2. Recruiter Workflow

Step 1: Company talent partners register with company details and designations.
Step 2: Recruiter accounts initially hold PENDING status until verified by the institutional T&P cell.
Step 3: Once approved, recruiters post job vacancies with required skills, minimum CGPA, eligible branches, and stipends.
Step 4: Screen applicants, filter by merit/skills, schedule multi-round interviews, and confirm offers with atomic selection transactions.

3. Administrator / T&P Cell Oversight

Verification Gatekeeper: Reviews incoming recruiter profiles and approves new job vacancies before they go LIVE.
Academic Management: Configures departments, academic branches, and master skill taxonomy.
Analytics & Compliance: Monitors live conversion funnels, department benchmarks, salary distributions, and exports audit-ready CSV reports.
